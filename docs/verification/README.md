The six Surah sheets contain page-heading crops from the supplied scan, labeled with the 1-based PDF ordinal. Three ornamental cover crops and the closing supplication also appear; these were excluded from chapter mappings. The other 113 chapter headings were read and matched in sequence by their Arabic names. At-Tawbah has no Bismillah band and was reviewed separately in [the full source page 169](../../assets/quran-pages/9985b515f88a/169.webp).
